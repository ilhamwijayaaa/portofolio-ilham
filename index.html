About Me

Passionate about Ecological Preservation and Technology.

I am a highly motivated Forestry Engineering student with a profound interest in Reforestation Technology, Spatial Analysis, and Biodiversity Monitoring. I strongly believe that tackling today's complex environmental challenges requires innovative, data-driven solutions combined with traditional ecological knowledge.

My field experiences range from evaluating bamboo reclamation on post-mining sites to mapping shoreline dynamics and conducting flora/fauna inventories. I am proficient in GIS tools (ArcGIS, QGIS) and love creating web-based inventory systems to digitize environmental data.

Beyond fieldwork, I am highly proficient in geospatial software and statistical tools. I also possess a strong passion for web development—specifically creating web-based inventory systems to digitize, manage, and disseminate vital environmental data effectively for better decision-making.
